Key Scientific Limitations

  1. Local Holdout vs. Hidden Test Ground Truth: Local spatially-blocked hide-and-recover evaluation (gems57-pooled-hide-v2) withholds mapped USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue fault blocks (22,893 positive pixels across 8 folds), whereas the DrivenData leaderboard evaluates predictions against both mapped faults and newly compiled blind/unmapped geothermal faults. Consequently, HOLDOUT-DTI (0.05264) and ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED leaderboard DTI (0.2778) measure different ground-truth densities and must never be conflated or projected onto one another.
  2. Coarse Native Resolution of Geodetic & Seismic Grids: In training_features.tif, geodetic strain rates (geod_shearrate, geod_dilaterate, geod_2ndinv) and earthquake density grids (ieq_n100a15, deq_n100a15) are regional (~5–15 km) fields interpolated onto the 100 m grid. They provide regional tectonic regime weighting rather than 100 m fault-core localization.
  3. Incomplete 1 m LiDAR Swath Coverage: The 1 m LiDAR scarp bands (lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif) cover priority fault valleys and geothermal play fairways rather than 100% of the Great Basin footprint; our S_scarp formulation smoothly falls back to 100 m det_elev and det_elev_slope where LiDAR swaths are zero.
